# WexumVPN failure and privacy matrix

**PROPOSED acceptance behavior.** Each test ID resolves to a scenario in [the lab plan](LAB_MIGRATION_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md). None of these lab scenarios has run. “Deny” means refuse the affected new enrollment, issuance, admission or renewal. It never implies that the OS actually blocked cleartext traffic. A Gateway cutoff, native disconnect, route cleanup and UI state require separate observations.

**Existing sessions during authority loss.** “R08 pending” means CTO/Security must choose, bound and verify their disposition before running a scenario that relies on continued forwarding. No offline grace or SLA is assumed. When protection evidence becomes unknown, remove the verified claim. Conclusive expiry, compromise or revocation triggers enforcement. Its completion requires separate evidence. An unavailable authority does not prove that such an event occurred.

## R07/R08 failure observations

**PROPOSED.** The following limits are acceptance expressions pending approval. **A** means stop by loss of required current authority + `T_detect_max + T_cutoff_max`. **B** means stop by the original online anchor + `T_lease_max + T_cutoff_max` or an earlier binding expiry/restriction. **C** means physical cutoff within `T_cutoff_max` of a locally known restrictive trigger. Use the earliest applicable trigger. Include source freshness in the budget: positive authority challenges must prove current management/key/generation state and cannot reset the budget using cached upstream facts. Neither an ACK timeout nor `T_reconcile_max` buys additional forwarding time. Stop timing uses calibrated error intervals under R09. Here “evidence” means the indicated record **and** independent continuous synthetic ingress/egress for every packet claim. No observer means NOT VERIFIED. Rows describe the affected trust scope.

**Minimum incident telemetry.** Restrict a per-run opaque correlation ID, kind of operation/failure, authority epoch/revision, peer generation/boot alias, policy version, phase, clock-error bracket, aggregate marker counts and artifact digests to QA/Ops reviewers. Keep lookup mapping and raw synthetic observations in a separate access-controlled lab store until the agreed audit/deletion point. Publish sanitized categories, counts, time intervals and hashes only. Exclude real user IPs, user traffic contents, private DNS queries, secrets, payment details, private keys and valid production permits from evidence. Validate redaction before publication and include backup deletion or fencing in teardown.
