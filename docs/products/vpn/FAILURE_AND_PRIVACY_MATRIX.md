# WexumVPN failure and privacy matrix

**PROPOSED acceptance behavior.** Each test ID resolves to a scenario in [the lab plan](LAB_MIGRATION_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md). None of these lab scenarios has run. “Deny” means refuse the affected new enrollment, issuance, admission or renewal. It never implies that the OS actually blocked cleartext traffic. A Gateway cutoff, native disconnect, route cleanup and UI state require separate observations.

**Existing sessions during authority loss.** “R08 pending” means CTO/Security must choose, bound and verify their disposition before running a scenario that relies on continued forwarding. No offline grace or SLA is assumed. When protection evidence becomes unknown, remove the verified claim. Conclusive expiry, compromise or revocation is an explicit enforcement event. An unavailable authority does not prove that such an event occurred.

## Privacy checks and recovery evidence

| Surface | Necessary handling | Acceptance evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity and device Control | Restrict opaque actor/device records, public-key bindings and deny revisions to the authoritative store. Exclude login secrets, raw assertions and personal attributes from logs. | Seed synthetic credential markers. Inspect errors, traces and crash outputs. Test account deletion, revoke propagation and restored backups. | #34/#2 |
| Billing, issuer and Gateway | Keep provider receipts within Billing. Separate signer audit, transient permit replay IDs and aggregate node counters. Avoid raw permits, end-user IPs, packet contents, DNS names and destination labels. | Confirm known synthetic canaries absent in ordinary telemetry. Inspect admission and rotation error paths. | #9/#3/#8/Gateway |
| Lab observers | Capture only controlled probes at independently configured vantage points. Restrict temporary synthetic traces. Publish scrubbed summaries/digests with run identity and limitations. | Demonstrate a deliberate synthetic bypass is observable, a missing sensor yields incomplete, and teardown removes local artifacts at the approved deletion point. | #10/#11/#78 |
| Retention and incidents | Approve a purpose, access group, expiry/deletion trigger, legal constraint and backup treatment per class. Preserve restrictive revocation information through reconciliation. | Exercise deletion and restore without reactivating a deleted subject. Record who reviewed evidence and when raw traces can be destroyed. | CTO/#8/#11 |

The isolated lab has synthetic subjects and controlled endpoints only. No operational leak-prevention capability or production retention interval has been established.
