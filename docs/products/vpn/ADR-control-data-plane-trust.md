## Problem statement

Six independent prototypes can each accept a locally valid value while the system still lacks an authenticated end-to-end connection. The integration must establish who can enroll a device, which node can accept its session, what happens when that authority changes, and what measurements justify a protection claim.

## Goals and non-goals

Deliver contracts and falsifiable acceptance specifications for one native client, one isolated Gateway and synthetic accounts. Production billing, public APIs, deployment, a release verdict, multi-region scale and product implementation require separate owner work. This package contains no wire standard or operational guarantee.

## Constraints and trust boundaries

Identity owns verified subjects and sessions. Control owns tenant-scoped device state. Billing owns provider evidence. Entitlements owns eligibility. Registry owns inventory. The Gateway owns local resource preparation and forwarding. The proposed VPN authority owns the order of admission and allocation against the peer's fenced capacity budget. Clients own their OS boundary. Each service authenticates its caller and authorizes the exact operation. Hub is an optional scoped client. No cross-domain database reads. The packet worker receives only the session policy it needs. [Domain boundaries](https://github.com/Dimash313/Wexum/blob/ead392f9fd823814f35916823b0a477d571427c5/docs/architecture/BOUNDARIES.md) · [Threat model](https://github.com/Dimash313/Wexum/blob/7b97c64bb2459d60ede1ab03fe85011aaafe824c/docs/vpn/security/THREAT_MODEL.md)

## Decision proposal

1. **Control Plane.** Verify the login at a trusted Identity edge, resolve membership server-side and authorize every owner-scoped operation. Separate eligibility to enroll from eligibility to connect. Complete a device-key challenge before binding an active device. Record a revision on activation or revocation. A caller-supplied actor, device row or receipt cannot confer access.
2. **Management Plane.** Enroll the Gateway peer under a controlled trust root. Bind its current registration generation to that peer. Authenticate observation writers, authorize targets and reject stale generations. Distribute versioned configuration, revocations and public verifier material. Keep incident access and category-only metrics outside the packet process.
3. **Issue and admit.** Select a candidate node before naming its audience. An isolated signer rechecks current authority and binds tenant, subject, device key, session, node and generation. At each new admission or renewal, obtain fresh online authorization, validate the permit locally and atomically consume admission capacity and the attempt's replay state. Treat the authoritative admission commit as the ordering point against revoke. The Gateway can hold a tentative slot while it waits, but can only forward after it has the confirmed decision for that exact attempt. Revoke winning the race denies admission. Revoke after the commit follows J, even if the reply was lost. On an ambiguous reply, reconcile the original attempt. A partition or cache cannot establish new authority.
4. **Data Plane.** The authenticated Gateway verifies the peer proof, signature, scope, freshness, binding, revocation and replay state before enabling the reviewed engine. Identity and Billing stay outside the packet path. Reconnect requires a fresh admission. Handle existing sessions under a separately approved outage rule.
5. **Protection evidence.** Authenticate status from the privileged native adapter. Bind OS tunnel, engine session, attempt, generation, entitlement, device, current network path and routing/DNS evidence. Drop the UI protection claim on stale/conflicting evidence. Observe packet behavior independently to accept the lab slice. [Sequences](COMPONENTS.md) · [Boundary contracts](INTERFACES.md)

## Considered alternatives

| Choice | Recommendation for the lab | Benefit, cost and condition for revisiting |
|---|---|---|
| Single-process prototype or separate trust principals | Colocate ordinary control modules on one isolated host if helpful. Separate signer, management identity, Gateway and native privileges | Simplifies lab operations while limiting authority after compromise. Splitting every control module into a network service adds failure modes before there is load evidence. |
| Centralized introspection, wholly offline permits or hybrid admission | Fresh control decision at admission/renewal plus node-local signature and binding checks | Central-only increases dependence on the control service and leaves node identity/capacity checks necessary. Offline admission trades availability for stale authorization. Hybrid adds one admission round trip and a reconciliation obligation. |
| Signing key on nodes or isolated signing authority | Give nodes verifier material only | Limits minting power of a compromised node. Requires key distribution, acknowledgement, incident invalidation and a recovery drill. A compromised Gateway can still mishandle its own traffic. |
| Client first, backend simulator first or integrated vertical slice | Build trust and deny paths first, then one real engine and native client | A simulator can verify contracts cheaply but cannot validate OS routes. An early client-first demo has no trustworthy peer. Android has APK evidence. Desktop offers controllable Linux observation points but lacks privileged IPC and runtime route evidence. |
| Live payment or isolated fixture | Seek separate approval for a server-only synthetic lab authority | Avoids provider accounts, costs and personal data. Evidence applies only to synthetic eligibility. Production acceptance requires a real provider validation and reconciliation review. |

## R08/J · Losing authority during an active session

**PROPOSED · PENDING APPROVAL.** A partition can leave the data path fully usable. Both options require a fresh online decision for admission, reconnect, renewal or a new peer generation. Either stops earlier on explicit deny, loss of binding, invalidated keys, untrusted time or upstream expiry. A client that loses evidence clears verified status, applies its approved OS isolation policy and requests fresh authority before reconnecting. An authority outage and an underlay route leak are separate observations.

**Authority and deadlines.** Only the fenced R07 authority can certify a live session at a committed `(authority_epoch, revision)`. Its authenticated reply answers a fresh Gateway challenge and covers the exact session, device, tenant, peer, generation, applied deny floor, trust profile and limiting expiries. It checks current account, device, subscription, management generation and key state before answering. A TCP heartbeat or an alive-but-lagging replica cannot refresh the deadline. Anchor the budget at challenge **send** on a trusted, suspend-aware elapsed clock. Reject expired, delayed, reused or reordered responses. Translate signed absolute limits conservatively using the earliest plausible expiry. Consume uncertainty inside the budget. Advancing wall time may shorten authority, rolling it back cannot extend it.

| Event | Timestamp and witness |
|---|---|
| Loss and detection | QA brackets last good exchange and partition injection at `t_loss`. Independent local watchdog declares missed fresh authority by `t_detect`. Late or unsent challenges cannot postpone this deadline. |
| Decision to stop | `t_decide` records the applicable trigger and revision. In A, start shutdown on detection without discretionary grace. In B, detection freezes the pre-existing limit and expiry triggers shutdown. An explicit restrictive decision preempts either. |
| Packet cutoff | `t_cut` is the physical transition after which the affected path no longer forwards. QA brackets it at the calibrated probe resolution using continuing ingress, the last matching egress and sustained missing egress. Include queue drain, both directions, route variants and offload. A completed delete call proves only local action. |
| Receipt and reconciliation | `t_ack` records authenticated receipt arrival, possibly long after cutoff or never. Unknown persists until evidence matches session, revision, generation and boot. Reconciliation has its own deadline after authority returns. |

| Comparison | A · no offline grace | B · finite pre-authorized continuation |
|---|---|---|
| Security / trigger | Allow forwarding only while an independent fail-closed gate holds a current positive authority confirmation. Missed deadline closes it. | The same gate may honor one finite lease issued **online before loss** by the fenced authority through the approved signing profile, for an already-active session only. |
| Availability | Brief outages can interrupt every active session. | Some established sessions survive short outages. A lease close to expiry may give no extra time. |
| Delayed revoke | A revoke just before or during partition prevents every later positive confirmation. Withholding the revoke cannot refresh the last allowance. | Revocation stops immediately on verified receipt, otherwise the remaining original lease creates a known exposure window. A reconnect cannot consume that window. |
| Time and maximum exposure | Detect by `t_loss + T_detect_max`. Complete cutoff by `t_detect + T_cutoff_max`, including decision latency. Worst case from the last pre-revoke authorization is their sum. | Bind immutable issue anchor, absolute expiry, tenant, subject, device key, session, permit, peer and registration/boot generation. Stop by earliest upstream expiry or last online lease anchor + `T_lease_max`, plus at most `T_cutoff_max` for physical cutoff. Remaining exposure from revoke is at most remaining lease + cutoff. |
| Restart / storage | Default-deny gate before interfaces can forward. Invalidate boot state and old allowances. Corruption or loss requires a new generation and online bootstrap. | Same boot fence. An encrypted disk copy cannot establish elapsed time or freshness. A restored, copied, expired or tampered lease is unusable after reboot. No resume from disk. |
| Implementation | Challenge freshness, independent timer and fail-closed enforcement across every forwarding path. | All of A's enforcement and time checks plus signed lease binding, anti-rollback and proof that neither retries, transport rekeys nor partition transitions can move expiry. |
| Operations | Frequent authenticated checks, authority capacity, reconnect bursts and false-cutoff monitoring. | Less disruption but additional issuance, secure-time monitoring, lease inventory and incident tracing. |
| User experience | Withdraw stale protection immediately. Reconnect with a fresh intent after recovery. | A still-forwarding session may be displayed as unverified if evidence is stale. Expiry stops it even if the OS still reports connected. |
| Incident response | An online deny closes the gate. If Control is unreachable the local guard expires. Escalate missing enforcement evidence to Ops. | Same explicit deny/independent isolation. Remote emergency recall cannot reach a partitioned node. Budget for the full remaining lease. |
| Testability | Measure missed confirmation through last packet with watchdog/process failures and queued traffic. | Also pin absolute expiry before partition; test early/late partition, renewal, replay, rollback and boot discontinuity. |
| Rollback | Freeze positives, close gates and reconcile all denies before restarting readers. | Freeze issuance, fence peers or wait out every outstanding bound with packet evidence. Never translate old leases into a fresh allowance. |

**Recommendation.** Prefer A for the first measured slice. It gives Security a smaller revocation exposure and QA fewer assumptions to validate. Consider B only after the additional availability benefit is measured against the maximum residual forwarding, with separate CTO and Security acceptance. B cannot renew locally, start another connection, migrate between peers or recover an expired lease. Restoring the channel requires fresh trust, current deny state and authorization before any new allowance. A recovered session that already closed requires a new admission. A challenge cannot use an older Management response to move the deadline forward. If a required source cannot give fresh evidence, use the oldest still-valid authority anchor until the gate closes.

**Watchdog ownership and fault envelope.** The future CTO-assigned Gateway Runtime owner must deliver an independently expiring forwarding gate. Ops owns external isolation and alerts. Proposed mechanism: the packet path checks a non-persistent, fail-closed allowance with an absolute deadline even if the verifier, refresh loop or userspace watchdog hangs. A hung worker may leave kernel or offloaded forwarding alive. Boot defaults, both directions and accelerated paths must obey the same fence. If the local timer/gate fails, a separately trusted host or network isolation actuator must cover all egress within the same bound to include that fault in a guarantee. Without an independent working timer/actuator, or on a compromised host able to bypass all gates, **no finite cutoff can be guaranteed**. Fence outside the host, record enforcement as unknown, and block acceptance for that fault model. Storage compromise alone cannot mint authority. Suspected key or runtime compromise requires isolation, credential rotation and new enrollment.

| Pending parameter | Measurement required before CTO + Security can sign |
|---|---|
| `T_detect_max` | Bound challenge cadence, validity, transport delay, replay rejection, suspend and scheduling under supported peak load. Verify last good to detection during total and asymmetric partitions. |
| `T_cutoff_max` | Bound trigger to last observable packet including decision, programming, buffering, offload and independent failover. Exercise process freeze, timer loss and partial teardown in both directions. |
| `T_lease_max` | Compare residual exposure after revoke at the earliest lease instant against measured availability benefit. Prove no path can increase signed expiry, even after restore. Applies only if B is selected. |
| `T_clock_uncertainty` | Bound drift, synchronization, observer alignment, probe spacing, suspend and timestamp error. Subtract uncertainty from usable authority. If uncertainty cannot be bounded, deny/stop. |
| `T_reconcile_max` | Measure trusted link recovery to complete snapshot/delta, key sync, local apply, stop evidence and receipts at maximum backlog. Exceeding it quarantines the peer without extending R08. |

Budgets cover the declared hardware, failure set and operating envelope. Measure upper bounds including instrumentation error, declare unsupported cases and test controls. A percentile alone cannot establish a hard maximum. The outage bound measures actual forwarding. It gives no bound on receipt arrival while a channel is partitioned.

## R07 · One order for admission and restrictive changes

**PROPOSED.** Use one linearizable VPN authorization history for the first lab. Identity, Billing, Device and Registry keep ownership of domain facts. Their authenticated owners submit version-checked restrictions to the VPN authority. For a restrictive domain mutation, fence VPN authorization first, then finalize the domain change. A domain failure after that fence leaves access denied. On timeout report pending/unknown and reconcile the same operation. For a permissive mutation, finalize the source proof first and require a separate version-checked VPN commit. Failed workflows cannot compensate a restriction with an automatic allow. Asynchronous notification by itself leaves an unbounded source-to-VPN gap. If a domain cannot couple its restrictive decision to that fence, admissions and positive heartbeats require a current fenced eligibility decision or must close. Report detection of an external refund separately from its effective VPN revision. Enforce known subscription expiry directly at commit and on the local gate even if no expiration event arrives.

| Design choice | A · one transactional store, synchronous lookup | B · one authoritative order, durable outbox and local apply (recommended) |
|---|---|---|
| Order / concurrency | Serialize guarded admission, capacity and revoke in a linearizable transaction. Every check reads the current leader. A separate local action still opens or closes the peer. | Same central commit orders decisions. Persist the restrictive record, revision and outbox atomically. An authenticated per-peer stream carries that order across process and network failures. |
| Infrastructure and partition | Fenced writer, synchronous reads from Gateway and periodic inspection. Loss of authority denies new work; existing traffic follows R08. | Add relay, local durable deny state, apply receipts and snapshot service. Loss of either freshness or the sole writer blocks new work; R08 limits already-active sessions. |
| Retries / retention | Durable scoped intent and outcome query. Retain consumed permits, denies and intent records across the entire replay/restore window. Missing reply triggers lookup. | Same keys plus immutable event ID, ordered stream and per-generation watermark. Retry outbox delivery until reconciled or fenced. Preserve denies even when delivery succeeds. |
| Recovery / scale / cost | Simple for one lab node. Polling and synchronous reads grow with peers. Reconstruct by authoritative lookup after every boot; tracing actual enforcement needs extra work. | One serialization domain initially limits write throughput. Batch dispatch and per-peer streams isolate slow consumers. Restore checkpoint + suffix, then reconcile enforcement. More storage, monitoring and operational work. |

Choose B for the lab proposal because the operator must distinguish a durable revoke from observed enforcement and recover a lost reply. B uses A's transactional ordering. Neither architecture can linearize a remote packet with a database commit. For future multi-region writes, compare a quorum-backed linearizable log with a single fenced home region. Independent writable regional SQLite copies cannot establish this order.

**Transaction and activation.** Create or find the scoped intent and immutable input digest. A durable preparation grants no forwarding. Revalidate domain revisions, effective expiry, writer epoch, selected peer generation, proof and permit in the serialized admission transaction. Atomically consume the permit, allocate one abstract slot, bind a session, increment revision, record outcome and append outbox. Concurrent deny transactions serialize through the same writer; a winning deny makes the admission fail. A later deny terminates the already-committed right under J/R08. Before first forwarding, require a fresh challenge-bound online activation decision for the same intent, live boot and current revision, then arm the local gate once under its local deny lock. A late commit reply alone cannot open a gate. A revoke after the activation decision can still race physical enablement; include that interval in exposure. If the requirement is literally zero packets after central revoke commit, neither design demonstrates that guarantee.

**Store boundary.** #55 implements SQLite WAL, owner-scoped device rows, hashed enrollment keys, a composite unique request key, bounded lock handling and `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` around limit check, insertion and owner revoke. #81 adds active-first bounded listings and supporting indexes. Its candidate #67 ran 24 Control tests including concurrent device limit, duplicate/conflicting enrollment, revoked retry, lock/open errors and active history. Those transactions cover devices only. No ordered tunnel ledger, cross-domain fence, outbox, recoverable admission, host fencing, secure time or packet-stop receipt was found. [Store at #81](https://github.com/Dimash313/Wexum/blob/03130999c36d0b68f9994df01a584005d3c3d051/products/vpn/control/src/wexum_vpn_control/store.py) · [Test log review](https://github.com/Dimash313/Wexum/pull/67#issuecomment-7075815590)

**Failure domain and recovery.** For a one-host lab, route all mutations and fresh reads to one fenced database service on durable local storage. Use serializable write transactions, checked durability and uniqueness on the same file. Independent workers may contend for that writer, never authorize from their own replicas. No automatic promotion or shared network filesystem. On leader ambiguity, fence both candidates and stop issuing confirmations. A writer epoch from an independent non-rollbackable authority and a certified complete deny history are prerequisites for failover. A newer epoch cannot by itself recreate lost denies.

Keep an authenticated off-host checkpoint and durable ordered journal sufficient to reconstruct **every** effective commit, including a revoke whose reply was lost. Verify continuity to an independently trusted latest committed floor before unlocking. If that floor or complete tail cannot be established after disaster, quarantine restored data, fence all old generations/credentials and keep affected identities denied pending authoritative reconstruction. Taking the largest of two stale counters is insufficient. The single-file lab can safely remain unavailable after disk loss. Releasing a slot requires evidence of closure or a verified generation fence that prevents the prior session from forwarding. A reservation timeout alone cannot release active capacity for reuse. ## Security and privacy tradeoffs

A short-lived capability limits exposure but duration alone cannot bind a device, prevent replay or communicate revocation. Online admission improves revocation observability but loses availability during partitions. Store the minimum transient admission state required for deduplication and incident cutoff. Separate this from product analytics. Never export tokens, secrets, destinations, queries, packet contents or payment data in ordinary logs. Lab path measurements use controlled synthetic endpoints and restricted, scrubbed evidence with an owner and deletion condition. ## Failure assumptions

Expect lost, duplicated, delayed and reordered messages, unavailable storage, clock faults, peer recreation and asymmetric partitions. A response can disappear after an operation committed. Preserve revocations across retry, restart and rollback. Unknown authority denies new connections. Existing-session behavior on lost control remains BLOCKED for approval. The UI can withdraw a claim without proving packets stopped. [Failure and privacy matrix](FAILURE_AND_PRIVACY_MATRIX.md)

## Consequences and rollback

The first slice depends on synchronous authority at admission, one isolated verifier and a real OS observation rig. Losing any prerequisite can reduce availability. Before implementation, closing this docs PR withdraws the proposal. In the future lab, stop issuance and new admissions, invalidate lab roots and leases, stop the engine, verify route and DNS restoration and destroy synthetic state. A rollback must retain evidence of prior revocation until the environment is destroyed.
