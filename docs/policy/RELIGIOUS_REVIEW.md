# IhsanSoft religious and ethical review policy

The project owner requires avoidance of haram and doubtful features under a Salafi approach. A general-purpose tool is not automatically an endorsement of every user action. Equally, known direct facilitation of prohibited activity cannot be ignored. Product decisions must be reviewed honestly, not inferred from an AI's unsupported claim to issue a fatwa.

## Default exclusions
Do not deliberately build products for gambling/raffles, riba-based lending, pornography, prohibited sexual content, music entertainment, deception, trafficking, fraud or knowingly illicit activity. Do not introduce music, depictions of living beings or questionable AI media outputs into the default product design.

## Requires qualified review before shipping
AI image/video generation, depiction of living beings, media editing, public social discovery, ads and marketplaces, potentially objectionable media distribution, financial products, user-generated-content discovery and platform monetization details.

## Process
1. Each issue records its purpose, expected use cases, direct capabilities, likely misuse, payments and data flow.
2. Flag doubtful capabilities; disable them by default and block release pending review.
3. Provide precise product facts and primary-source religious references to a qualified scholar appropriate to the user's requested methodology. Record the opinion, scope, uncertainties and decision.
4. Technical team implements documented restrictions and appeal/review workflows; it does not declare religious judgments independently.
5. Revisit decisions when capabilities or business models change.

## Privacy and reality
Private encrypted user data cannot be exhaustively inspected while honestly claiming end-to-end privacy. Focus on product intent, prohibited built-in functions, transparent published-content rules and abuse reports, rather than promising impossible complete surveillance.
